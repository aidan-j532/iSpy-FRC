BIG PICKLE — Phase: Close AGPL in-process leaks + replace bundled default
models with boot-time downloads

CONTEXT
Decision made: iSpy will NOT ship _default_detect.pt, _default_pose.pt, or the
v26 fuel-detect model as bundled assets in the repo/package anymore, because
their AGPL status (per Ultralytics' own licensing FAQ, which asserts that
trained/fine-tuned YOLO models are AGPL-3.0 by default regardless of who
trained them or what data was used) is unresolved and conflicts with shipping
the rest of iSpy under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Instead, these three
models are downloaded from external URLs at boot time, the same way
QuantizeDataset calibration images already are (see
iSpy/dataset/dataset.py::_download_release_images and
_CALIBRATION_RELEASE_URL/_VALIDATION_RELEASE_URL for the existing pattern to
mirror — same retry/session/timeout conventions, same "never crash boot on a
failed download" philosophy).

I (Aidan) will supply the actual download URLs. Do NOT guess or invent URLs.
Leave them as an explicit, clearly-labeled placeholder constant for me to fill
in before this ships — treat a missing URL as a hard stop with a clear log
message, not a silent skip.
