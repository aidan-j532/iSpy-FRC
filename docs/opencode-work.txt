BIG PICKLE PROMPT — Final licensing audit + cleanup before public release

CONTEXT
iSpy is releasing publicly under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Prior work
established: (1) Ultralytics (AGPL-3.0) must never import in-process at
runtime — subprocess isolation via _convert_worker.py and
_yoloworld_reparam_worker.py handles this; (2) the two stock Ultralytics
checkpoints (_default_detect.pt, _default_pose.pt) are being moved to
download-on-demand from Ultralytics' own release URLs instead of being
bundled in the repo; (3) RKNN-Toolkit2 wheels mirrored on our GitHub
releases are BSD-3-Clause (confirmed, redistribution permitted with
attribution); (4) the bundled calibration dataset ZIPs
(200.Robotics.Images.zip, 200.Human.Images.zip, valid.zip) are being
DELETED — their provenance couldn't be fully cleared (YouTube frames,
unclear Kaggle CCTV dataset license) and the owner has decided to drop
them rather than resolve provenance.

This is a final verification + cleanup pass, not new feature work. Your
job is to audit the whole repo against these decisions and either fix
clean mechanical gaps or report anything requiring a human judgment call.
DO NOT invent new licensing text. DO NOT guess at unclear provenance and
