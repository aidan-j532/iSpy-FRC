# iSpy-FRC Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

iSpy-FRC is a community project intended to make FRC vision systems more accessible, reliable, and useful.

We want contributors to feel comfortable asking questions, reporting problems, proposing ideas, and sharing their work. Everyone participating in the project should be treated with respect, regardless of experience, background, or level of involvement.

## Expected behavior

Project participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Give constructive, technically grounded feedback.
- Discuss disagreements without personal attacks.
- Respect different experience levels and technical perspectives.
- Give appropriate credit for other people's work.
- Respect copyright, licensing, and contributor ownership.
- Avoid pressuring people into unpaid work or unwanted commitments.
- Accept reasonable feedback and correct mistakes where practical.
- Keep project discussions focused and useful.
- Respect other contributors' time, privacy, and boundaries.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Criticism of code, designs, performance claims, and project decisions is appropriate when it remains focused on the issue rather than attacking the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Discriminatory or demeaning attacks against people or groups.
- Repeated insults, personal attacks, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexually abusive conduct.
- Knowingly misrepresenting another person's work or authorship.
- Deliberately removing required copyright or license notices.
- Pressuring contributors to accept licensing terms without a reasonable opportunity to review them.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a good-faith concern.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would recognize as threatening, abusive, or seriously disruptive.

Respectful criticism, licensing questions, and disagreement with maintainers are not violations simply because they are uncomfortable or challenge a decision.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and communication channels officially maintained or used by iSpy-FRC.

It also applies to project-related events and spaces where someone is acting as a representative of the project.

Maintainers may consider conduct outside these spaces when it directly affects participant safety or the integrity of the project community.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately through a reporting method published in the repository.

Include enough information to explain what happened. Links, dates, and a description of the behavior may help.

Do not include passwords, private keys, or unrelated sensitive information.

If a report concerns a maintainer, or you do not feel safe contacting the person involved, use another available project contact if one exists. The project should establish an appropriate alternative before claiming that independent escalation is available.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to investigate or address a concern.

Reports should be made in good faith. Knowingly fabricated reports and retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report may themselves violate this Code.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for applying this Code fairly and proportionately.

Depending on the circumstances, a response may include:

1. A private request to stop the behavior.
2. A warning explaining the concern.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation in a discussion or project space.
4. Removal of abusive content where appropriate.
5. Temporary suspension or permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Responses should consider the seriousness of the conduct, its impact, relevant context, and whether the response is likely to resolve the problem.

A person directly involved in a complaint should not be its sole decision-maker. Where practical, another uninvolved person should review the matter.

Maintainers may take immediate action when necessary to protect participants or prevent ongoing harm.

## Fairness and appeals

Where practical, people affected by an enforcement decision should be told the reason and given an opportunity to provide relevant context.

If an appropriate uninvolved reviewer is available, a person may ask that reviewer to reconsider the decision.

The project should not promise an independent appeal process unless one has actually been established.

This Code must not be used to suppress respectful criticism, good-faith licensing questions, or disagreement with project decisions.

## A shared responsibility

Maintainers and contributors share responsibility for a constructive community.

Maintainers should communicate clearly, apply expectations consistently, and avoid using their position to intimidate contributors. Contributors should engage respectfully and recognize the time, rights, and boundaries of others.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct guides participation in iSpy-FRC. It does not replace applicable law, the project's software license, or separate contributor agreements.
